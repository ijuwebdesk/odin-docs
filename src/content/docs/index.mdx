## What Silk does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Voice companion" icon="comment">
    Hold a key, speak, and Silk hears you — no typing needed. It sees your screen and answers with spoken audio.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Screen aware" icon="laptop">
    Silk captures your display and reads the context of whatever you're looking at before responding.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Cursor overlay" icon="right-arrow">
    A blue triangle points directly at the element Silk is talking about, so you always know exactly where to look.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Background agents" icon="rocket">
    Tell Silk to research, write code, or run multi-step tasks — it spins up a headless agent and reports back when done.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
