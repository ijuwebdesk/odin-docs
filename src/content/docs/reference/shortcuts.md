## Global hotkeys

These work anywhere on your system, even when Silk's panel isn't open or focused.

| Action | macOS | Windows |
|---|---|---|
| Push-to-talk: press once to start listening, press again to send | `Ctrl+Space` | `Ctrl+Space` |
| Open or hide the Silk panel | `⌃⇧Space` (Control+Shift+Space) | `Ctrl+Shift+Space` |
| Stop a running task (Act Mode or an agent at work) | `Escape` or `⌘.` | `Escape` or `Ctrl+.` |

You can change the push-to-talk hotkey in **Settings → Voice hotkey**. See [Push-to-Talk](/features/push-to-talk/#changing-the-hotkey).

The panel shortcut is the quickest way back to Silk when its icon is hidden in a full menu bar or system tray.

## Stopping Silk while it controls your computer

While Silk is clicking and typing for you, any of these stops it immediately:

- Press **`Escape`** (or **`⌘.`** / **`Ctrl+.`**).
- **Move the mouse yourself.** Silk assumes you're taking over.
- **Push the mouse pointer into the top-left corner** of the screen and hold it there for a moment.

:::note
**`Escape` doesn't hide the cursor overlay.** It only stops a running task. To hide the overlay, click **Hide Overlay** at the bottom of the Silk panel.
:::

## Panel controls

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Open or close the panel | Click the Silk icon in the menu bar or system tray, or press the panel shortcut above |
| Open settings | Click the gear icon in the panel |
| Start a fresh conversation | Click **Clear context** in the panel |
| Show or hide the cursor overlay | Click **Hide Overlay** / **Show Overlay** at the bottom of the panel |
