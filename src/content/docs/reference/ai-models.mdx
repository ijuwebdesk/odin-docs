Silk works with any model on OpenRouter that supports image input and tools, and from Silk 1.1.79 it can also run on your [ChatGPT subscription](/getting-started/chatgpt-subscription/). So a common question is: which one should I use? Public leaderboards don't answer that well. They test puzzles and chat, not the work you hand Silk: organizing folders, filling in web forms, researching vendors, updating a CRM, fixing code, and following the skills and memories you've taught it.

So we built our own benchmark. It runs Silk's real agent, with the same instructions, tools and safety checks that ship in the app, on the kinds of tasks people actually use Silk for. Then it checks the results the way you would: did the work get done, done correctly, and done without breaking anything?

## Our recommendations

You can change your model any time in **Settings → Model ID**. Copy the ID exactly as shown.

| Model | Model ID | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash** | `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` | **The default and our top pick.** Passed all 62 runs, the fastest of the accurate models (median 20 seconds per task), about 1 cent per task. |
| **GPT-6 Luna** | `openai/gpt-6-luna` | **The backup.** 97% of runs passed, the cheapest per successful task, and from a different provider, so it's a good switch if DeepSeek is ever unavailable. |
| **Qwen 3.7 Plus** | `qwen/qwen3.7-plus` | **The dependable last option.** The previous default: accurate (97%), but slower and about twice the cost per task. |
| **MiMo V2.6 Pro** | `xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-pro` | **For unattended work.** Also passed every run, but about 5 times slower, so it suits scheduled and long background jobs where you aren't waiting. |

**Also capable, but not our first choice:** GLM 5.3 FlashX (`z-ai/glm-5.3-flashx`, 97% but about twice DeepSeek's cost), MiniMax M3 (`minimax/minimax-m3`, 95% but slower and pricier), and GPT-5.6 Luna (`openai/gpt-5.6-luna`, 94%; GPT-6 Luna does better for less).

**Not recommended right now:**

- **StepFun Step 3.7 Flash** overwrote or lost files in three separate runs, once while reporting success.
- **Gemini 3.1 Flash Lite** is the fastest model tested, but it failed 18% of runs, including scheduling a meeting at 5 AM for one attendee and not saving CRM changes.
- **Qwen 3.8 Flash** is capable when it can be reached, but its provider throttled it heavily during our tests.

<Aside type="tip">
**If Silk feels slow or makes mistakes,** check which model you're on before anything else. Switching to `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` fixes a lot of "Silk is slow" and "Silk got it wrong" problems.
</Aside>

## What else we found

- **Every model resisted a prompt-injection attack.** One test page hid instructions telling the AI to send a confidential file to an attacker. None of the ten models did it, and in most runs the model also warned the user about the suspicious page.
- **Silk's own features work well across the board.** Memory, skills, MCP integrations and scheduling were handled correctly by 7 of the 10 models in every run. Every OpenRouter model that finished the test asked "which Dana?" before issuing a refund when two customers shared that name. (On a ChatGPT Free or Go plan, the smaller model didn't. See [below](#on-a-chatgpt-plan).)
- **Long, exact jobs separate the models.** The hardest task was updating exactly 7 of 30 leads in a CRM. Only 11 of 20 runs got it fully right: some models skipped a lead, changed one they were told to leave alone, or wrote notes without saving the status changes.
- **Speed matters in practice.** DeepSeek's median of 21 seconds on the hard tasks compares with 76 seconds for Qwen 3.7 Plus and 138 seconds for MiMo. Over a day of tasks, that's the difference between Silk feeling instant and feeling slow.

## How we tested

- **The real agent.** Every run used Silk's Background agent with its built-in tools (files, shell, browser, web search, PDFs, memory, skills, schedules) and its "did you really finish?" checks.
- **Real-world tasks.** 31 tasks in three groups: everyday work (sorting folders, filling forms, summarizing data, SEO audits, fixing code, writing a flyer), hard multi-step jobs (supplier sync, CRM follow-up, invoice filing, messy data, time-zone scheduling), and Silk's own features (memory, skills, MCP, schedules, asking before irreversible actions).
- **Fair and repeatable.** Test websites were served locally so every model saw identical pages, and each model ran every task twice: 620 runs on OpenRouter, plus 94 on ChatGPT plans.
- **Graded on outcomes.** A run passed only when the result was fully correct. Speed and cost were measured on successful runs only, and costs are what we were actually billed.

## On a ChatGPT plan

From Silk 1.1.79 you can [power Silk with your ChatGPT subscription](/getting-started/chatgpt-subscription/) instead of an OpenRouter key. We ran the same benchmark through it on three ChatGPT plans in October 2026, using the model Silk recommends for each plan.

**ChatGPT Plus, full benchmark (62 runs), next to the best OpenRouter models:**

| Model | Passed | Median time | Cost per task |
|---|---|---|---|
| DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (OpenRouter) | 62/62 (100%) | **20s** | $0.011 |
| GPT-6 Luna (OpenRouter) | 60/62 (97%) | 38s | $0.009 |
| **GPT-6.1 Sol (ChatGPT Plus)** | **62/62 (100%)** | 54s | **Included in your plan** |

**ChatGPT Free and Go, Silk features suite (16 runs each):**

| Plan | Model | Passed | Median time | Allowance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | GPT-6 Luna | 14/16 (88%) | 24s | About 300 tasks a month |
| Go | GPT-6 Luna | 14/16 (88%) | 41s | About 400 tasks a month |

What this means:

- **Plus matches the best OpenRouter model on quality.** It passed every task, including asking "which Dana?" before a refund, in both runs.
- **It's slower.** A typical task took about 2.7 times as long as DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Choose ChatGPT mode to avoid a separate AI bill, and OpenRouter when speed matters most.
- **Plus is generous.** All 62 tasks used 40% of one 5-hour window and 6% of the weekly allowance: about 150 tasks every 5 hours, or 1,000 a week.
- **Free and Go are a step down.** They only offer the smaller GPT-6 Luna, and both failed the same test twice: they refunded an order without asking which of two customers named Dana was meant. Everything else passed. The slower Go time most likely reflects server load when we ran it, not the plan.

## Limits of this benchmark

- **It covers Silk's Background agent,** which handles most real usage. Guide mode (voice answers with on-screen pointing) and live Act mode (Silk clicking and typing on your screen) weren't part of this test.
- **Models were tested in October 2026.** Models, providers, prices and ChatGPT plan allowances change often, and we plan to re-run the benchmark as new models arrive.
- **Your workflows may differ.** If a model isn't working for you, try another from the recommended list.

## Free models

OpenRouter also lists free models (their IDs end in `:free`). Silk can use one if it supports **image input** and **tools**, but free models are rate-limited and less accurate, so expect slower and less reliable results on screen tasks. For most people, a paid model with a [credit limit](/getting-started/first-setup/#step-1--get-an-openrouter-api-key) on the key is the better way to keep costs low: usage on the recommended models is around a cent per task, and the limit means you can never be charged more than you set.
