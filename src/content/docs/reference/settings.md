Open Settings by clicking the **gear icon** in the Silk panel, or **Settings** in the top bar of the [app window](/features/app-window/). Settings are grouped into the pages below; both layouts show the same settings.

## AI engine

Choose what powers Silk: your **Claude** plan, your **ChatGPT** plan, or an **OpenRouter** key.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Power Silk with** | Claude, ChatGPT or OpenRouter. See [First Setup](/getting-started/first-setup/). |
| **Account** | Claude and ChatGPT only: switch between two signed-in accounts, or add a second one. See [Claude](/getting-started/claude-subscription/#using-a-second-claude-account) or [ChatGPT](/getting-started/chatgpt-subscription/#using-a-second-chatgpt-account). |
| **Model** | Claude and ChatGPT only. Leave it on the recommended model unless you have a reason to change. |
| **Effort** | Claude and ChatGPT only: how long the model thinks on each step. Higher effort uses more of your plan. |
| **Plan limits** | Bars showing how much of your Claude or ChatGPT plan's limits you've used. |
| **OpenRouter API Key** | OpenRouter only: the key Silk uses. Paste it with the **Paste** button. |
| **API spend** | OpenRouter only: how much this key has spent today. |
| **Model ID** | OpenRouter only: which model Silk uses. The default is `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`; see [Choosing an AI Model](/reference/ai-models/). |

## Voice & audio

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Speaking** | Turn Silk's spoken replies on or off. Replies always appear as text too. |
| **Sound when tied off** | A short sound when a task finishes. Nothing else in Silk makes a sound. |
| **Language** | The language you speak to Silk in, and the voice it answers with. [More about languages](/features/push-to-talk/#languages). |
| **Voice** | The voice Silk speaks with. [Community members](/features/community/#premium-voices) can pick and preview premium voices, and see their monthly usage. |
| **Microphone** | Which microphone Silk listens to, with a test: the level bars should move when you speak. |

## Background tasks

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Background task folder** | Where [background tasks](/features/background-agents/) save their files unless you say otherwise. |
| **Spending** | OpenRouter only: the most one task (**Each task**) or one scheduled run (**Scheduled tasks**) may spend before it stops and asks. See [Spending caps](/features/background-agents/#spending-caps). |
| **Helpers per task** | Off, 2 or 3 (default). How many [helpers](/features/background-agents/#helpers) work on parts of one task at the same time. Paid plans only. |
| **Stay reachable** | **Keep this computer awake when plugged in**, so scheduled tasks and tasks from your phone always reach it. On Windows it also wakes the PC for scheduled tasks. See [Keeping your computer reachable](/features/scheduled-tasks/#keeping-your-computer-reachable). |

## Connect a phone

Pair Silk with **Telegram** so you can send it tasks, files and voice notes from your phone, and **Unpair** to disconnect. See [Telegram Bridge](/features/telegram/).

## Silk Browser

The Chrome profile your tasks use to sign in to websites: its status, **Set up** / open it to add logins, **Sign in to Google**, and **Use for tasks** to switch it on or off. See [The Silk Browser](/features/silk-browser/).

## Look & feel

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Text size** | Makes Silk's text larger or smaller. |
| **Appearance** | Dark or light theme. |
| **Detached window** | Off: Silk lives under its icon in the menu bar or system tray. On: Silk opens as a full [app window](/features/app-window/) with a Dock or taskbar icon. |
| **Show Silk's cursor** | Show or hide Silk's blue [cursor overlay](/features/cursor-overlay/). |

## Hotkeys

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Voice hotkey** | The push-to-talk shortcut, `Ctrl+Space` by default. Click it and press a new combination; **Reset** restores the default. See [Push-to-Talk](/features/push-to-talk/#changing-the-hotkey). |
| **Typing** | The shortcut that opens the type bar: a double-tap of **Shift** by default. See [Typing to Silk](/features/typing/). |
| **Speak typed replies** | Off by default, so typed requests get text-only replies. Turn on to hear them too. |

## Advanced

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **High-accuracy pointing** | Off by default. Turn it on if you work in design or custom-drawn apps (Photoshop, Figma and similar) where the [cursor overlay](/features/cursor-overlay/) doesn't land exactly on the right element. It uses a more powerful model only when needed, so it costs a little more. |
| **Tips, events & community news** | Occasional cards about live trainings, the community and new features. Turn off to hide them; important notices still show. |
| **Share usage stats** | On by default. Sends counts and timings, linked to your licence email so we can help when setup gets stuck. Never your screen, recordings, prompts or files. Turn it off to stop sending. |

## Permissions and License

- **Permissions**: check and re-grant **Microphone**, **Screen Recording** (Mac) and **Accessibility** (Mac). In the panel, click the **shield icon** or choose **Permissions…** from the menu-bar or tray menu. See [Permissions Setup](/getting-started/permissions/).
- **License**: your plan, **Refresh license**, **Deactivate this PC**, and **Cancel subscription** on the monthly plan. See [Activation & License](/getting-started/activation/).

In the app window, both are pages in the Settings menu, along with the version (click it to copy your version and support code), **Check for updates** and **Quit Silk**.
