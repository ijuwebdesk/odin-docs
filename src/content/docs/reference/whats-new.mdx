Silk updates itself in the background; restart Silk when it says an update is ready. See [Updating Silk](/reference/troubleshooting/#updating-silk).

## Silk 1.1.85 (November 13, 2026)

- **Google sign-in handled for you.** When a task runs into a Google sign-in, Silk opens the [Silk Browser](/features/silk-browser/#signing-in-to-google) at Google's sign-in page and carries on once you've signed in, instead of looping.
- **[Helpers](/features/background-agents/#helpers).** A background task with separate parts can split them between helpers that work side by side, so it finishes sooner. Choose Off, 2 or 3 in Settings → Background tasks.
- **[Stay reachable](/features/scheduled-tasks/#keeping-your-computer-reachable).** Keep a plugged-in computer awake so scheduled tasks always run. Windows PCs also wake up for scheduled tasks.
- **Second ChatGPT account.** Sign in with two ChatGPT accounts and switch between them in Settings when one hits its limit. See [Use Your ChatGPT Subscription](/getting-started/chatgpt-subscription/#using-a-second-chatgpt-account).
- **Announcements** about live trainings and new features appear on Home and, occasionally, as a card. Turn them off in Settings → Advanced.
- The app window: **Quit Silk** in the Settings menu, and formatted results on the Tasks page.
- Fixes: a website's *"Leave site?"* prompt can no longer freeze a task, the permissions window can be moved on Windows, and follow-up tasks show their own plan.

## Silk 1.1.84 (November 8, 2026)

<YouTube id="XPVzynPKdtA" title="Silk 1.1.84: Silk Is Now a Full Desktop App" />

- **[The Silk app window](/features/app-window/).** Silk can run as a full desktop app with Home, Tasks, History, Projects, Schedules, Library and Settings. Turn on Settings → Look & feel → Detached window.
- **[Type to Silk](/features/typing/).** Double-tap Shift anywhere to type a request, with Tab to pick Guide, Act or Background.
- **[Attach files everywhere](/features/typing/#attaching-images-and-files).** Paste, drop or attach images and files in every chat box.
- **[The Silk Browser](/features/silk-browser/).** Tasks now use your own Chrome on a separate Silk profile, so saved passwords, password managers and passkeys work. Tasks can also upload files to websites.
- **New voices.** The standard voices for other languages were replaced, and [community members](/features/community/#premium-voices) can preview and pick premium voices.
- **Second Claude account.** Sign in with two Claude accounts and switch between them. See [Use Your Claude Subscription](/getting-started/claude-subscription/#using-a-second-claude-account).
- **Plan usage bars** for Claude and ChatGPT in Settings.
- Settings reorganised into pages: AI engine, Voice & audio, Background tasks, Connect a phone, Silk Browser, Look & feel, Hotkeys and Advanced. See [Settings](/reference/settings/).

## Silk 1.1.83 (November 1, 2026)

- **[Drawing on your screen](/features/drawing/).** Guide can sketch over your screen in 2D, or build 3D models you can turn, paced to what it's saying.
- **[Suggestion cards](/features/suggestions/).** Silk offers one useful task for the app or site you're in, and learns from your own repeated tasks. Mac and Windows.
- **[Silk community](/features/community/).** A separate monthly membership with premium voices and one-click Connected apps (Gmail, Sheets, Calendar and many more).
- **Effort setting** for Claude and ChatGPT, so you can trade speed for more careful thinking.
- **Faster, cheaper Guide.** Guide sends far less to the AI on each request.
- Background tasks make up to 25 web searches each.
- Your conversations are now saved the moment they start, so a crash can't lose them, and Silk survives freezes and hard restarts without losing data.

## Silk 1.1.82 (October 26, 2026)

- **[History & Projects](/features/history-projects/).** Search past conversations, continue any of them, and group work into projects with their own instructions and memory.
- **[Monthly plan, cancel in the app](/getting-started/activation/#monthly-subscription-renewing-and-cancelling).** Cancel your subscription from the License page; Silk keeps working until the paid period ends.
- **The Agent Dock** can be dragged anywhere and collapsed, and long questions scroll.
- **GoHighLevel** connections use GoHighLevel's newer server automatically. See [MCP Servers](/features/mcp-servers/).
- **A new setup video** with highlights in the window.
- Fixes: long text wraps and links are clickable in History, and tasks working through long lists are no longer stopped by mistake.
