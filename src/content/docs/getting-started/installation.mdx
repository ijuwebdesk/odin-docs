## Download

Download the latest version of Silk from [download.heysilkai.com](https://download.heysilkai.com).

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="macOS">

**Requirements:** macOS 12 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel.

1. Download the `.dmg` file for your architecture:
   - `Silk-<version>-arm64.dmg` — Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4)
   - `Silk-<version>.dmg` — Intel
2. Open the `.dmg` and drag **Silk** into your Applications folder.
3. Launch Silk from Applications.

<Aside type="tip">
If macOS shows "Silk cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified", right-click the app and choose **Open** instead of double-clicking. This only needs to happen once.
</Aside>

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Windows">

**Requirements:** Windows 10 or later (64-bit).

1. Download `Silk Setup <version>.exe`.
2. Run the installer. Windows SmartScreen may show a warning — click **More info → Run anyway** to proceed.
3. Follow the installer steps. Silk can be installed per-user (no admin required).
4. Launch Silk from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.

<Aside type="note">
The SmartScreen warning appears because Silk's Windows installer is not yet signed with a trusted certificate. A code-signing certificate is in progress and will be available within the next week, after which the warning will no longer appear. The installer is safe to run.
</Aside>

  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## First launch

When you first open Silk, it walks you through a short onboarding flow to grant the permissions it needs:

1. **Microphone** — required for push-to-talk voice input.
2. **Screen Recording** — required on macOS for screen capture and AI analysis.

You must grant both permissions before Silk can function fully. See [Permissions Setup](/getting-started/permissions/) for details.

After permissions are granted, Silk's icon appears in your **menu bar** (macOS) or **system tray** (Windows). Click it to open the companion panel.

## Updating

Silk updates itself automatically in the background. When a new version is available, it downloads silently and applies on the next launch. You can check your current version in the panel settings.
