## Step 1 — Get an OpenRouter API key

Silk uses [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai) to access AI models. OpenRouter is a single API that gives you access to hundreds of models from different providers (Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral, and many more) under one key and one bill.

<Steps>
1. Go to [openrouter.ai](https://openrouter.ai) and create an account.
2. Open the **Credits** section and **add credits** (the minimum top-up is around $10). OpenRouter is pay-as-you-go — you're only charged for what Silk actually uses.
3. Navigate to **Keys** and click **Create key**. Give it a name (e.g. "Silk").
4. Optionally set a **credit limit** (e.g. cap the key at $10/month) and an **expiration** date. Leave them blank for no limit.
5. Copy the key — it starts with `sk-or-v1-…`.
</Steps>

<Aside type="tip">
Setting a monthly credit limit on the key is a good way to stay in control of spend — the key stops working once it hits the cap and resets each month.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
**Can't tick the "agree" box on OpenRouter?** That's a problem on the OpenRouter website, not in Silk. Try a different browser (Chrome or Edge), or zoom out with `Ctrl` + `-` (`⌘` + `-` on Mac) in case the checkbox sits off-screen.
</Aside>

### What does the AI cost?

Silk is **bring your own AI engine**: your purchase is the Silk software, and the AI "brain" runs on your own OpenRouter account. OpenRouter bills you directly for what you use; Silk never charges you for AI usage.

- **It's usage-based, and small.** With the default model, a typical task costs around a cent. A credit top-up lasts a long time for everyday use.
- **The minimum top-up is OpenRouter's**, not a Silk fee, and it isn't a subscription.
- **Other AI subscriptions can't be used.** Silk connects only to OpenRouter, so a ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot subscription can't power it.
- **You're always in control.** A credit limit on the key (step 4 above) means you can never be charged more than you set.

## Step 2 — Enter the key in Silk

<Steps>
1. Open the Silk panel (click the Silk icon in your menu bar or system tray).
2. Click the **gear icon** to open Settings.
3. Paste your key into the **OpenRouter API Key** field.
4. Click **Save**.
</Steps>

Silk stores the key locally on your device. It's only ever sent to OpenRouter's servers when making AI requests.

<Aside type="tip">
**Can't paste the key?** Click the **Paste** button next to the API key field, or right-click inside the field and choose **Paste**. If neither appears, [update Silk](/reference/troubleshooting/#updating-silk) first: older versions didn't have them.

If Silk says the key is invalid, it has usually picked up an invisible space. Copy the key again with OpenRouter's **copy** button rather than selecting the text by hand, and paste it with no space before or after.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
**Never share your API key** with anyone, including Silk support: we will never ask for it. If you've pasted it into an email or chat, delete that key in OpenRouter and create a new one.
</Aside>

Silk's setup video waits on this step: it continues once a valid key is saved. If the video doesn't move on, the key hasn't been saved yet.

## Step 3 — Choose a model

In Settings, the **Model ID** field controls which model Silk uses. This is a free-text field — type any model ID from OpenRouter's catalogue.

**Default model:** `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash`, the fastest model with a perfect score in our [October 2026 benchmark](/reference/ai-models/), at about a cent per task.

### Recommended models

| Model ID | Notes |
|---|---|
| `deepseek/deepseek-v4.1-flash` | **Default and top pick.** Passed every test task; fast and inexpensive. |
| `openai/gpt-6-luna` | **The backup.** Nearly as accurate and the cheapest per task. A good switch if DeepSeek is ever unavailable. |
| `qwen/qwen3.7-plus` | **The previous default.** Accurate, but slower and about twice the cost. |
| `xiaomi/mimo-v2.6-pro` | **For unattended work.** Very accurate but slow; best for scheduled and long background tasks. |

The Model ID field suggests the first three as you type. For the full results, and the models we don't recommend, see [Choosing an AI Model](/reference/ai-models/).

<Aside>
Model IDs follow the format `provider/model-name`. Copy the ID from the table above and paste it directly into the Model ID field in Silk's settings.
</Aside>

### Changing the model

You can switch models any time in Settings — just type a new model ID and click Save. The change takes effect on the next push-to-talk query.

## Step 4 — Review the other settings

Below the API key, a few more settings are worth a quick look:

- **High-accuracy pointing** — turn this on when you work in precise tools (Photoshop, design apps) so the [cursor overlay](/features/cursor-overlay/) lands exactly on the right element.
- **Anonymous usage data** — on by default. It's fully anonymous (no screenshots, recordings, or prompts) and only used to improve Silk. Toggle it off any time to opt out.
- **Audio / microphone** — open the audio section and make sure the correct microphone is selected. Use the **test** button to confirm the input bars move when you speak. If Silk can't hear you (or won't speak back), this is the first thing to check.

Click **Save** and your settings are stored.

## Step 5 — Try it

Silk's push-to-talk is **press-to-toggle**, not hold:

1. Press **`Ctrl+Space`** once to start listening.
2. Speak your question.
3. Press **`Ctrl+Space`** again to stop — Silk then processes and responds within a few seconds.

If nothing happens, check:
- The key is saved correctly (the field shows dots, not empty)
- You have credits on your OpenRouter account
- Microphone permission is granted (shield icon in the panel)
- The correct microphone is selected in the audio settings
