The **Silk community** is a membership on Skool, separate from your Silk license, for **9 dollars a month**. Members get live trainings and help from other Silk users, and two extras inside the app. You can join from the **Join** button in Silk (Library → Connections → Connected apps) or at [cliqkio.com/r/skool](https://cliqkio.com/r/skool).

<Aside type="note">
The community isn't part of any Silk plan, including lifetime licenses. It's its own monthly membership. If you cancel, the perks keep working until the end of the month you've paid for.
</Aside>

## Premium voices

Members can choose from a large set of natural-sounding **premium voices** in **Settings → Voice & audio → Voice**, and preview each one before picking it. Members also get extra spoken languages.

Each computer gets about **200,000 characters** of premium speech a month (roughly several hours of replies). The usage line under the voice picker shows how much is left. After that, Silk switches to the standard voice until next month.

## Connected apps

Connected apps let Silk work directly in your Gmail, Google Sheets, Drive, Docs, Calendar, Slack, Notion, HubSpot, Trello and many more, with **one-click sign-in** and no API keys or servers to set up.

<DemoClip id="1246170884" title="Connected apps in Silk" />

<Steps>
1. Open **Library → Connections** in the [app window](/features/app-window/) (or the **MCP servers** window from the panel) and choose the **Connected apps** tab.
2. Find the app, using search if you need to, and click **Connect**.
3. Sign in to that app in the window that opens and allow access.
4. Ask Silk to use it: *"Check my email for anything from Dana"*, *"Add these leads to my Sales sheet"*, *"What's on my calendar tomorrow?"*
</Steps>

Your connected apps are listed at the top of the tab, with usage for the month.

Not a member? You can still connect apps yourself with [MCP servers](/features/mcp-servers/), and Gmail and Calendar tasks work through the [Silk Browser](/features/silk-browser/) once you've signed in to Google there.

## Perks not showing?

- **Check the email.** Your Skool email may be different from the email on your Silk license. Tell us both when you contact support.
- Go to **Settings → License** and click **Refresh license**, then restart Silk.
- Membership can take a few minutes to arrive after you join on Skool.
