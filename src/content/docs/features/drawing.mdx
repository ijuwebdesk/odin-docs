When you ask Silk something in Guide mode, it can **draw** its answer over your screen while it talks. Each part of the drawing appears in step with what Silk is saying.

## 2D: marking up your screen

Silk can circle, frame, highlight and point at things on your real screen, and draw paper cards on top of it with notes, step-by-step flows and simple bar charts.

<DemoClip id="1246170853" title="Silk drawing over the screen in 2D" />

Try:

> "Walk me through this dashboard and circle what matters."

> "Sketch how this funnel flows, step by step."

> "Compare these three plans for me."

## 3D: models you can turn

For physical things (an engine, a bridge, the inside of a phone) Silk builds a **3D model** piece by piece while it explains it. It can cut parts away or make shells see-through so you can look inside, and label each part.

<DemoClip id="1246170852" title="Silk drawing a 3D model" />

- **Drag** the model to turn it.
- **Scroll** to zoom in and out.
- Click the **close** button when you're done.

Try:

> "Draw me how a car engine works, in 3D."

## When Silk draws

- Ask for it with words like **draw**, **sketch**, **show me** or **in 3D**. In **Auto** mode these requests always go to Guide.
- Silk also draws without being asked when a picture explains it better, for example a comparison.
- Physical objects are always drawn in 3D.

## Closing a drawing

A drawing closes when you press **Esc**, when you start talking to Silk again, when you switch to another app (on Mac), or on its own after a while.

<Aside type="note">
Drawing uses the same AI engine as the rest of Guide, so there is nothing extra to set up. Silk's blue [cursor](/features/cursor-overlay/) follows the pen while it draws.
</Aside>
