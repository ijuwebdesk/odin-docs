If you're on an **agency plan**, Odin gives you a dashboard for creating and managing licenses for your clients. You provision a seat, send the client a personalized invite, and revoke access whenever you need to — all from one place.

<Aside>
The Agency Dashboard is only available to agency users. If you open it without an agency plan, Odin takes you to the upgrade page instead.
</Aside>

## Opening the agency portal

<Steps>
1. Open the Odin app and click the **Agency Dashboard** icon in the panel.
2. Odin redirects you to the agency portal in your browser.
3. If you aren't logged in, enter your email. The portal sends a sign-in link to that address — click it to reach the logged-in dashboard.
</Steps>

## Provisioning a client license

<Steps>
1. In the dashboard, enter your **client's email address**.
2. Give the seat a **label** so you can identify the client later.
3. Click **Provision**. Odin creates an account for that client.
</Steps>

Once provisioned, the client has their own Odin account tied to the email you entered.

## Sending the invite

Each provisioned seat has a **personalized invite** — an invite link pre-filled with the client's email. Click the copy button next to the client to copy it, then send it to them however you like.

## Revoking access

To remove a client, click the **revoke** button on their row. This deactivates their access and frees the seat.

## Seat usage

The dashboard shows how many seats you've used out of your total (for example, **used out of 100**), so you always know how much capacity you have left before you need to add more.
