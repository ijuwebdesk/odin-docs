From Silk 1.1.84, Silk can run as a **full desktop app** in its own window, not only as the small panel under the menu-bar or tray icon. It's the easiest way to use Silk if you prefer typing, or if you like to watch your tasks while they work.

<DemoClip id="1246170877" title="The Silk app window" />

## Switching between the window and the menu bar

<Steps>
1. Open Settings and go to **Look & feel**.
2. Turn on **Detached window**. Silk opens in its own movable window with a Dock (Mac) or taskbar (Windows) icon.
3. To go back to the small panel, click **Dock to menu bar** at the top right of the window, or turn **Detached window** off again.
</Steps>

Everything you set up (skills, memory, schedules, projects, your license and your AI engine) is the same in both. Only the layout changes.

## What's in the window

The bar across the top takes you to each part of Silk:

| Page | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Home** | A **What should Silk do?** box to type or speak a request, with Auto, Guide, Act or Background, a project picker and a mic. Below it: **Running now**, **Waiting for you**, **Pick up where you left off** and **Coming up** (your next schedules). |
| **Tasks** | Every background task, with live progress. Pick one to see each step it took, its plan, the files it made and its result. Start a new one with **New background task**. |
| **History** | All your past conversations. See [History & Projects](/features/history-projects/). |
| **Projects** | Group related work, with instructions and its own memory. See [History & Projects](/features/history-projects/#projects). |
| **Schedules** | Create and manage [scheduled tasks](/features/scheduled-tasks/). |
| **Library** | **Skills** (things Silk knows how to do), **Memory** (what Silk remembers about you) and **Connections** (apps and MCP servers Silk can use). |
| **Settings** | All of Silk's settings in one place. See [Settings](/reference/settings/). |

When you talk to Silk with push-to-talk, or type in the window, the conversation appears in a chat view, including Act Mode's live steps and any question Silk needs you to answer. Click **Stop** to end a task.

## Live task progress

A background task shows what it's doing while it works: its plan, the step it's on, how much it has spent, and any files it has produced. Files have **Open** and **Show in Finder** (or File Explorer) buttons. If Silk needs an answer from you, the task waits under **Waiting for you** on Home until you reply.

## Version, updates and support code

At the bottom of the Settings menu you'll find:

- **Silk vX.Y.Z**: click it to copy your version and **support code**. Paste both into a support request so we can look into your issue faster.
- **Check for updates** (or **Restart to update** when a new version has downloaded).
- **Quit Silk**.

<Aside type="tip">
Closing the window doesn't quit Silk. It keeps running in the menu bar or tray so push-to-talk, the type bar, Telegram and your schedules keep working. Use **Quit Silk** in Settings, or the menu-bar icon's menu, to quit.
</Aside>
